Privacy, in plain words
  • Your files are read on your computer. All the maths happens in your own browser. Your Etsy files are never uploaded, to me or anyone else.
  • What's saved: only the product costs you type in and your currency, kept in your own browser so you don't have to retype them. You can delete them any time.
  • What's never collected: your files, sales, customer names or anything else from Etsy. There's no account, no database and no AI involved.
  • Visit counter: I count page visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you. It also counts when someone clicks "Try with sample data" or "Analyze my shop", so I know how many people use it. It only gets the name of the button, never anything from your files.
  • Feedback form: optional, it's a Google Form and doesn't collect your email.
  • Check it yourself: the full code is public on GitHub.

    How do I download these files from Etsy?
    1. Monthly statements: Shop Manager → Finances → Monthly statements → choose a month → Download CSV. Do this for each month you want (more months = better forecast; 12 months lets us detect your busy season automatically).
    2. Order Items (one file covers the whole year): Shop Manager → Settings → Options → Download Data → choose "Order Items", pick the year → Download CSV.

    Etsy sometimes moves these menus. If something looks different, look for "Download Data" or "Monthly statements".

    How is this worked out?

    These are estimates based on your shop's past fees. Fees that are a % of the price (transaction, payment processing, offsite ads) change with your price. Fixed ones (shipping labels, listing fees, your materials) don't. MakerLens never guesses how customers react: the "your guess" box uses your own estimate.
