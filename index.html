    How do I download these files from Etsy?
    1. Monthly statements: Shop Manager → Finances → Monthly statements → choose a month → Download CSV. Do this for each month you want (more months = better forecast; 12 months lets us detect your busy season automatically).
    2. Order Items (optional): Shop Manager → Settings → Options → Download Data → choose "Order Items", pick the year → Download CSV.

    Etsy sometimes moves these menus. If something looks different, look for "Download Data" or "Monthly statements".
